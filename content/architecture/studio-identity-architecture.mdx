Studios are not production units.  
Studios are **identity engines**.

Every great studio has:

- a creative signature  
- a systemic mastery  
- a tonal lane  
- a world logic  
- a cultural footprint  
- a continuity pattern  

But platforms rarely understand studios this way.  
They flatten studios into “developers,” losing the identity that makes their work meaningful.

Studio Identity Architecture defines how a platform recognizes, expresses, and sustains studio identity as a **structural system**, not a marketing label.

# STUDIO ≠ IP ≠ PRODUCT

Studios are not defined by:

- the IP they work on  
- the genre they operate in  
- the products they ship  

Studios are defined by **identity**, and identity is defined by **patterns**.

Coalition is precision combat and systemic clarity.  
id Software is kinetic mastery.  
Playground is world‑building precision.  
Rare is whimsical identity and cultural warmth.

Identity is structural.
