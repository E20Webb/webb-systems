# ECLIPSE — FICTIONAL IP OVERVIEW (DEMONSTRATION SETUP)

Eclipse is a **science‑fantasy action‑adventure universe** centered around planetary collapse, cosmic anomalies, and frontier expansion. It blends:

- traversal mastery  
- systemic combat  
- narrative world‑logic  
- evolving world‑states  

Eclipse is designed as a **continuity IP**, meaning its universe evolves over time through world‑state shifts, seasonal beats, and multi‑studio contributions. This makes it the perfect example for demonstrating **Release Cadence Architecture**.

---

# THE STUDIOS BEHIND ECLIPSE

Eclipse is built by **three studios**, each with a distinct identity and systemic mastery. Cadence relies on *studio rhythm* as one of its rails.

## Studio Solis — Traversal + Exploration Mastery

Solis defines the **physicality** of Eclipse — how players move through collapsing worlds and expanding horizons.

Solis specializes in:

- movement systems  
- traversal mechanics  
- environmental puzzles  
- frontier exploration  

Solis contributes through **seasonal traversal updates**, **frontier expansions**, and **exploration‑driven spinoffs**.

## Studio Ironfall — Combat + Systemic Clarity

Ironfall defines the **combat identity** of Eclipse — precision, clarity, and systemic depth.

Ironfall specializes in:

- combat systems  
- enemy logic  
- systemic encounters  
- mastery loops  

Ironfall contributes through **combat expansions**, **enemy evolutions**, and **high‑intensity tactical spinoffs**.

## Studio Lumen — Narrative + World Logic

Lumen defines the **mythology** of Eclipse — why the world changes, what the anomalies mean, and how the universe evolves.

Lumen specializes in:

- narrative arcs  
- world‑state evolution  
- cosmic anomalies  
- universe continuity  

Lumen contributes through **world‑state shifts**, **narrative arcs**, and **universe‑level cadence beats**.

---

# HOW THE STUDIOS RELATE TO ECLIPSE

Each studio contributes to Eclipse in a **cadence‑aligned way**:

- **Solis** → traversal cadence  
- **Ironfall** → combat cadence  
- **Lumen** → narrative cadence  

Together, they create a **multi‑studio cadence**, where each studio has its own rhythm but contributes to a shared universe evolution.

---

# THE SPINOFFS: VANGUARD & ECHO SQUAD

Spinoffs are essential for demonstrating **Series Cadence** and **Universe Cadence**.

## Eclipse: Vanguard — Tactical Exploration Spinoff (Studio Solis)

Vanguard is a **squad‑based tactical exploration game** built by Studio Solis.

It focuses on:

- anomaly scouting  
- frontier mapping  
- tactical traversal  
- small‑team decision making  

### Relation to Eclipse: Dawn

Eclipse: Dawn introduces the first major world‑state shift — the **Anomaly Bloom**.  
Vanguard explores the Bloom’s frontier zones, mapping anomalies that later become central to Eclipse II.

Vanguard is the **exploration cadence bridge** between Dawn and Fracture.

## Eclipse: Echo Squad — Combat‑Focused Spinoff (Studio Ironfall)

Echo Squad is a **high‑intensity combat tactics game** built by Studio Ironfall.

It focuses on:

- elite strike teams  
- anomaly‑infected enemies  
- systemic combat puzzles  
- mastery‑driven missions  

### Relation to Eclipse: Dawn

Echo Squad investigates the **Anomaly Bloom’s militarized zones**, revealing enemy evolutions that later appear in Eclipse II.

Echo Squad is the **combat cadence bridge** between Dawn and Fracture.

---

# HOW THE SPINOFFS RELATE TO THE MAINLINE SERIES

Both spinoffs are **cadence bridges**:

- **Vanguard** → exploration bridge  
- **Echo Squad** → combat bridge  

They deepen the universe between mainline entries and reinforce:

- World Cadence  
- Series Cadence  
- Studio Cadence  
- Platform Cadence  
- Community Cadence  

They also feed directly into Eclipse II’s:

- traversal systems (from Vanguard)  
- enemy logic (from Echo Squad)  
- narrative arcs (from Lumen’s world‑state evolution)
