Culture is not content.  
Culture is **continuity**, **identity**, and **shared meaning**.

Most platforms treat culture as a byproduct of content.  
But culture is a **system**, and systems can be architected.

Cultural Infrastructure Architecture defines the structures that allow:

- communities to form  
- identities to emerge  
- taste to propagate  
- shared moments to scale  
- creators to thrive  
- worlds to persist  
- platform identity to cohere  

Culture isn’t accidental.  
Culture is **engineered**.
