---

The industry still treats genre like a container.

Shooter.  
RPG.  
Fighting.  
Sports.  
Strategy.

These labels were useful when hardware was limited and games were simple. But today, genre is a **structural bottleneck** — it restricts creativity, misguides marketing, and misaligns player expectations.

Genre is a **legacy classification system** in a world that has outgrown it.

Players don’t think in genres.  
Players think in **experiences**.

Platforms don’t need genre.  
Platforms need **taxonomy**.

---

# THE GENRE PROBLEM

```text
GENRE PROBLEM
│
├── CREATIVE CONSTRAINT
│     Forces teams into predefined boxes
│     Limits experimentation
│     Discourages hybridization
│
├── MARKETING DISTORTION
│     Misrepresents tone
│     Misrepresents identity
│     Misrepresents audience
│
└── PLAYER MISALIGNMENT
        Genre ≠ experience
        Genre ≠ tone
        Genre ≠ play pattern
```

Genre is too broad to be useful and too rigid to be accurate.

---

# THE TAXONOMY MODEL

A modern platform needs a **taxonomy**, not a genre list.

Taxonomy breaks experiences into **atomic components** that can be recombined, analyzed, and personalized.

```text
TAXONOMY MODEL
│
├── LOOP
│     Duration
│     Intensity
│     Return pattern
│
├── TONE
│     Emotional arc
│     Aesthetic vibe
│     Narrative weight
│
├── SYSTEMS
│     Combat
│     Movement
│     Crafting
│     Progression
│
├── SOCIALITY
│     Cooperative
│     Competitive
│     Shared moments
│
└── IDENTITY
        World logic
        Aesthetic identity
        Cultural signals
```

This is how you classify experiences — not by genre, but by **structure**.

# THE GENRE COLLAPSE

Most modern games break genre entirely:

- Elden Ring: Action RPG? Adventure? Soulslike? Open world?  
- Fortnite: Shooter? Sandbox? Social hub? Live service?  
- Minecraft: Survival? Creative? Sandbox? Social?  
- Destiny: Shooter? MMO? RPG? Live service?  
- GTA Online: Action? Social? Sandbox? Economy sim?

Genre cannot describe these games.  
Taxonomy can.

# WHY TAXONOMY MATTERS

### 1. Better Discovery  
Platforms can recommend games based on **experience**, not genre.

### 2. Better Personalization  
Taxonomy plugs directly into the **Taste Graph**.

### 3. Better Creativity  
Studios can build hybrids without fighting genre expectations.

### 4. Better Marketing  
Games can be described by tone, loop, and identity — not outdated labels.

### 5. Better Platform Strategy  
Taxonomy reveals gaps, clusters, and opportunities in the catalog.

```text
SHOOTER →

RPG →

Systems: Stats + narrative + exploration
Tone: Dramatic / thematic
Social: Cooperative identity

Systems: Precision combat
Tone: Competitive tension
```

Genre is the **label**.  
Taxonomy is the **structure**.
