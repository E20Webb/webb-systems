## II. Why Now

Modern AI systems are powerful but fragile. They lack:

- **continuity across time** — no durable memory or long‑horizon context  
- **identity** — no stable self across devices, tasks, or environments  
- **accountability** — no governed substrate for safe autonomy  
- **multi‑agent coordination** — no shared environment for collaboration  
- **trust & explainability** — no platform‑level guarantees  
- **cross‑device persistence** — no unified place intelligence can operate  

AI has a brain.  
**It still needs a place to live.**
