## I. Context  
### Game Worlds Fail When Coherence Breaks Down

<div className="mt-8"></div>

Modern game worlds are living, interdependent ecosystems — narrative, behavior, identity, environment, economy, and time — evolving together. Yet today's tools fragment these systems across editors, scripts, spreadsheets, and engine‑specific pipelines.

As complexity grows, coherence collapses:

- authored intent drifts  
- systems contradict each other  
- simulation diverges from runtime  
- late‑stage changes cascade unpredictably  
- teams lose the ability to reason about the whole  

Studios spend enormous time and cost repairing coherence that should never have broken.

## II. Platform  
### What Forge Is — A Coherence Engine for Game Design

Forge is a systemic authoring platform designed to maintain coherence across design, simulation, and runtime.

It is **not** a scripting layer, not a visual editor, and not an engine extension.

Forge is a systemic authoring environment where designers define the logic, relationships, and behaviors of a world — and Forge ensures those definitions remain coherent from authoring → simulation → runtime.

**Forge provides:**

- deterministic world authoring — same inputs, same outputs  
- systemic definition of game logic — rules, not instances  
- simulation fidelity — authored intent behaves identically in simulation and runtime  
- engine‑agnostic portability — worlds bind to engines late, without drift  
- coherence guarantees — systems stay aligned as complexity grows  

Forge preserves the world you intended to build.

## III. Philosophy  
### Eight Principles of Coherent Worldmaking

Forge is built on eight architectural principles that ensure coherence at every stage:

- **Designer Sovereignty** — intent is primary and preserved  
- **Traceability** — every behavior has a clear, inspectable cause  
- **Determinism** — no hidden randomness, no silent divergence  
- **Reversibility** — change without fear; every step is recoverable  
- **Coherence** — systems remain aligned across layers and time  
- **Engine Independence** — author once, deploy anywhere  
- **Visibility** — designers see the whole system, not fragments  
- **Failsafe by Design** — coherence is protected even under failure  

Together, these principles guide how Forge maintains alignment across increasingly complex game worlds.
