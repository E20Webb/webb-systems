## II. Platform  
### What Forge Is — A Platform for Engine‑Independent Worldmaking

Forge is a designer‑first, deterministic, engine‑agnostic authoring platform that separates world logic from engine implementation.

**Forge provides:**

- systemic authoring — define world behavior once  
- deterministic simulation — validate logic before engine binding  
- late binding — attach worlds to engines at the end  
- cross‑engine portability — reuse systemic work across projects  
- coherence guarantees — no drift during integration  

Portability becomes a property of the world, **not the engine.**
