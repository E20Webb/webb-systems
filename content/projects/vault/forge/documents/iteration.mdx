## II. Platform  
### What Forge Is — A Deterministic Environment for Fearless Iteration

Forge is a designer‑first, deterministic, engine‑agnostic authoring platform that allows designers to iterate safely and confidently on systemic world behavior.

**Forge provides:**

- deterministic feedback — changes behave consistently  
- reversibility — every step is recoverable  
- systemic visibility — designers see the impact of changes  
- simulation fidelity — simulation matches runtime  
- coherence safeguards — systems remain aligned as iteration scales  

Iteration becomes a creative act, not a technical risk.
