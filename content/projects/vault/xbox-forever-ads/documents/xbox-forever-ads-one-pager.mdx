## I. Executive Thesis

<div className="mt-8"></div>

Create new revenue from new modes of play — not by degrading traditional games.

Xbox can monetize participation across discovery, competition, culture, and commerce while preserving player trust.

---

## II. The Opportunity

Gaming commands enormous attention, yet most advertising models were designed for other media.

They interrupt, redirect, and compete with the player’s purpose.

Xbox Forever Ads introduces dedicated, platform-native experiences where the economic model belongs — without rebuilding games around advertising.

---

## III. The Model

Xbox Forever Ads organizes monetization around three broad forms of participation:

- **Playable Discovery:** turn sampling into interaction and direct paths to play.
- **Competitive Spectacle:** create sponsorship around events, rivalry, and shared attention.
- **Live Culture:** connect highlights, creators, community activity, and relevant actions.

The point is not to insert more ads into games.

It is to create new experiences where the economic model belongs.

---

## IV. Platform Advantage

Xbox already brings together games, players, publishers, creators, identity, commerce, cloud infrastructure, subscription access, and decades of catalog value.

Value is created around the catalog:

- back catalog titles can participate
- current games can participate
- future releases can participate
- games do not need to conform to an ad-first design

**The platform creates new value around the catalog rather than requiring the catalog to conform to the revenue model.**

---

## V. Player Trust

The architecture is governed by one principle:

**Monetize curiosity, not frustration.**

That means:

- no interruption of traditional gameplay
- no coercive placement
- no monetization through artificial difficulty
- no reliance on sensitive personal inference
- clear safety and privacy boundaries
- advertising only where the mode itself supports it

The player experience remains the constraint.

Not the obstacle.

---

## VI. The System Behind It

Xbox Forever Ads is supported by a deeper economic and operating architecture spanning:

The public proposition is simple.

The underlying architecture supports discovery, culture, competition, creators, and commerce at platform scale.
