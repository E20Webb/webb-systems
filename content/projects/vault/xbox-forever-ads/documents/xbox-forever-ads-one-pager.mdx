    ## The Opportunity

    Gaming commands enormous attention, yet most advertising models were designed for other media: they interrupt, redirect, and compete with the player’s purpose.

    Xbox Forever Ads introduces dedicated, platform-native experiences where the economic model belongs—without rebuilding games around advertising.

    ## Platform Advantage

    Xbox already brings together games, players, publishers, creators, identity, commerce, cloud, subscription access, and decades of catalog value.

    Value is created around the catalog: back catalog, current, and future titles can participate without conforming to an ad-first design.

    ---

    ## Player Trust

    **Governing principle: Monetize curiosity, not frustration.**

    No interruption of traditional gameplay • no coercive placement • no artificial difficulty • no sensitive personal inference • clear safety and privacy boundaries • ads only where the mode supports them.

    ---

    **Economic and operating layer:** relevance • quality • auction logic • safety • delivery • pacing • telemetry • attribution • creator amplification • platform integration.
